Motivation

  • Classical simulation of quantum many-body systems scales exponentially (Hilbert space ∝ 2ⁿ)
  • Approximations fail for strongly correlated dynamics
  • Quantum computers emulate Hamiltonian dynamics directly
  • Applications: chemistry, materials science, nuclear & high-energy physics

Quantum Computing Stack

  • Applications: Quantum simulation, chemistry, optimization
  • Algorithms: VQE, Trotterization, analog/digital simulation

  • Control / Pulses: AWGs, pulse shaping, real-time feedback
  • Hardware: Trapped ions, motional modes, qubit readout
