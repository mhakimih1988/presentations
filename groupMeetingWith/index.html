Motivation

  • Classical simulation of quantum many-body systems scales exponentially (Hilbert space ∝ 2ⁿ)
  • Approximations break down for strongly correlated dynamics
  • Quantum computers can efficiently emulate Hamiltonian dynamics
  • Applications: quantum chemistry, materials science, nuclear & high-energy physics
  • Trapped ions provide high-fidelity gates and tunable interactions → ideal platform

References: Feynman 1982, Lloyd 1996, Aspuru-Guzik 2005, Georgescu et al. 2014, Blatt & Roos 2012

Quantum Computing Stack

  • Applications: Quantum simulation, chemistry, optimization
  • Algorithms: VQE, Trotterization, analog/digital simulation

  • Control / Pulses: AWGs, pulse shaping, real-time feedback
